Pressure definition

First innings: final five scheduled overs, or after halfway with at least five wickets down.

Chase: RRR ≥ 9; or ≤30 legal balls remaining with RRR ≥ 7; or ≤5 wickets remaining and RRR ≥2 above current run rate.

Pressure is evaluated immediately before each delivery.

Scoring method

Batting: pressure SR 25%, SR uplift 20%, dismissal rate 20%, boundary-ball rate 15%, dot-ball rate 10%, consistency 10%.

Bowling: pressure economy 25%, economy improvement 20%, wickets/24 legal balls 20%, dot-ball rate 15%, boundary-ball rate 10%, consistency 10%.

Sample adjustment: final score = 50 + (raw percentile score − 50) × confidence factor.

Batters: sqrt[(pressure balls/(pressure balls+200)) × (pressure matches/(pressure matches+10))]
Bowlers: sqrt[(legal pressure balls/(legal pressure balls+180)) × (pressure matches/(pressure matches+10))]

Minimum eligibility: batters ≥150 pressure balls and ≥10 pressure matches; bowlers ≥150 legal pressure balls and ≥12 pressure matches.

Data quality and limitations

835 IPL/T20I match records selected; 819 have delivery rows and 16 have none. The pressure score is a transparent statistical proxy, not a psychological-pressure or win-probability model. It does not fully adjust for venue, opposition quality, pitch or field settings. See the methodology PDF for complete calculations and limitations.
